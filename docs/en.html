CAW Social

Part of Guppy's Guide（グッピーズガイド） — Don't Panic — a hitchhiker's toolbox for 42 Chain & QL1

CAW is a social network that runs on the QL1 chain — no company in the middle. With this app you can read the timeline, and post, like and follow right from your phone.

⬇ Download for Android

Version v0.1.0 · Android

How to install

  1. Tap the download button above and save the app file.
  2. Open the file. Your phone may ask if it's OK to install it — that question is normal for apps installed outside the Play Store. Tap Allow or Install to go ahead.
  3. Open the app, then create a wallet or bring in one you already have. That's it — you can start reading and posting.

What you can do

  • Read the timeline — the newest posts, in order
  • Post, like, share and follow — all inside the app
  • Get your own name — how people find you on CAW
  • See profiles — a person's page with their posts
  • Look after your names and your balances

Good to know

Android only. On an iPhone? The web version works in your browser: CAW Social (web).

Not an official CAW app. This app is made by community members, with care, for everyone to use.

Free and open source. Everything is open under the MIT license — anyone can read the source code: source code on GitHub.

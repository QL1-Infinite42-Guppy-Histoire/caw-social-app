Privacy Policy

CAW Social is a community-made, non-custodial app for the CAW social network on the QL1 chain. It is published by Guppy's Guide (QL1-Infinite42-Guppy-Histoire) and is not operated by the official CAW team. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does with your information. It applies to the CAW Social Android app.

Last updated: November 8, 2026

The short version

  • Your wallet keys and recovery phrase are created and stored only on your phone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • We run no servers for this app, and there are no user accounts.
  • There is no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking in the app.
  • Things you do on the blockchain — posts, likes, follows, transfers, swaps — are public by design, like everything on a public blockchain.

Your wallet and keys

CAW Social is a non-custodial wallet app. When you create or import a wallet, your private key / recovery phrase is stored only in your device's secure storage, protected by the app password or your device's biometrics. The app never transmits your key or recovery phrase anywhere, and we cannot see, recover, or reset them. If you lose your recovery phrase, nobody — including us — can restore your wallet.

What stays on your phone

The following are stored only on your device and are never uploaded: your app settings and language, your address book, post drafts, the names you follow, and a profile picture if you pick one (the picture you choose stays on this phone only).

What leaves your phone, and why

To read the timeline and let you use your wallet, the app talks directly to public blockchain infrastructure. These services see your device's IP address and the wallet addresses involved in your requests, the same as with any blockchain app:

  • Blockchain nodes (RPC) on QL1, 42 Chain, BNB Smart Chain and Ethereum — to read balances, timelines and prices from on-chain data, and to broadcast transactions that you confirm.
  • Block explorer APIs (scan.qom.network and scan.infinitedrive.xyz) — to read post and transaction history.
  • A public IPFS gateway (ipfs.io) — to load profile images that are referenced on-chain.

We do not control these services and do not receive copies of your requests. Signed transactions you confirm are broadcast to the blockchain and become a permanent, public record — including the CAW you send, swap or bridge, and the posts, likes and follows you make. Never post anything on-chain that you want to keep private.

Permissions

  • Biometrics — only to unlock the app and confirm actions, if you enable it.
  • Photos — only the single picture you pick as a profile picture, through the system photo picker. The app does not scan your photo library.

Children

This app is a cryptocurrency wallet and social client. It is not directed at children, and it is not suitable for anyone who is not legally allowed to use cryptocurrency services in their country.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. The app is open source under the MIT license, so any change to how the app handles data can also be checked in the public source code.

Contact

Questions about this policy: please open an issue in the app's public repository — github.com/QL1-Infinite42-Guppy-Histoire/caw-social-app/issues. Source code: github.com/QL1-Infinite42-Guppy-Histoire/caw-social-app.
